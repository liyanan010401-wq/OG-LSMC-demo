# Release notice

This repository contains a self-generated synthetic CPU demonstration and
recorded synthetic outputs. It contains no patient scans, private MRI result
tables, confidential model checkpoints or full MRI training implementation.

The included models are PCA, ridge-regression and linear-denoising surrogates.
The demonstration and its tests do not reproduce the paper's clinical MRI
benchmark results. Third-party dependencies retain their respective licenses.
